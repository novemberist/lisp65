## What the release says

The proposed [v1.2.3 release notes](../releases/1.2.3.md) deliver:

- the native Dialect V2 `while` form, including the measured streamed-backedge
  refill property;
- unbiased, seedable `random` and `random-seed`;
- the generation-bound fast path for repeated `require`;
- interrupt-ownership hardening for Ethernet, Auto-IEC, and audio DMA; and
- the visible `WORKBENCH 1.2.3` banner.

The complete-name undefined-function diagnostic fixed in v1.2.2 is carried
forward unchanged.

The fast-path claim is deliberately split by evidence class. The reductions
of **97.96% VM steps** and **93.23% one-byte C2D reads** are host measurements.
Hardware proves repeat behavior and idempotence: after an asserted cold start,
two `(require 'place)` calls returned `t`, and the second left the C2D header
and `place` row byte-identical. Logical-analyzer markers 17–20 were not read on
the device, so no device timing claim is made.

## User-facing boundaries

Known Issues carries one new, plain-language entry: late in one long session,
`(require 'place)` returned `nil`; after a cold start it returned `t` twice.
Users are told to cold-restart and retry once, then preserve the library name
and preceding steps if it recurs. No cause or fix is claimed.

The remaining boundaries are carried forward:

- Freezer entry during an active persistent definition is unsupported;
- interrupt-generating cartridges are unsupported;
- the intermittent post-GC out-of-memory observation remains unreproduced;
- the fail-closed stop remains silent; and
- `defstruct`, dynamic package freight, `gc`, `room`, `error`, and
  `restart-repl` are not delivered.
