## Proposed policy

1. Tracked receipts, attributions, device captures and their bound identities
   are permanent and immutable.
2. A locally materialized archive required by an executable offline gate stays
   in the hot cache.
3. A superseded archive may leave the hot cache only if all of these hold:
   its identity remains in tracked evidence; an authoritative retrieval path
   is recorded and freshly verified; no current certification extracts it;
   and the owner explicitly approves the named deletion.
4. Generated build trees are disposable caches, but their cleanup is a
   separate workspace-maintenance action, not evidence retention.
5. No wildcard or age-based deletion is permitted.  The decision unit is one
   named archive with its byte count and SHA authority.

## Owner decision requested

The conservative recommendation is: **retain all five now**.  If 126.1 MiB of
immediate recovery is worthwhile, authorize only the v1.2.5 archive as a
conditional prune candidate; the deletion still waits for a recorded remote
retrieval/SHA verification.  The four r6 archives are not recommended for
deletion.
