# v1.6.0 E000 relocation study

**Date:** 2026-08-18

**Commission:** `3772232b`

**Result:** **A priced plan exists: move `c2_abort_driver`**

**Execution boundary:** host-only; zero compiles, links, media builds, product
source changes, or device contacts

The study finds one clean move. Relocating the 134-byte
`c2_abort_driver` body from the E000 window into the existing mapped Bank-2
far-service arena frees 134 E000 bytes. One new nine-byte entry replaces nine
bytes of the mapped-facade's existing explicit padding, so the fixed 98-byte
facade does not grow. After the already priced 59-byte input-capture seam is
placed, E000 retains **136 free bytes**: the 54-byte floor remains intact with
**82 bytes above it**.

The executable receipt is
`tests/bytecode/dialect-v2/evidence/architecture-blocks/c2.3-v1.6-e000-relocation-study-receipt.json`.

## 2. Why the selected move is legal

`c2_abort_driver` is 134 emitted bytes in `reopen_gap1`. ElfTruth finds exactly
one incoming transfer, from `c2_product_abort_cleanup`. The source establishes
that this landing is shared by ordinary errors and RUN/STOP cleanup. Its linked
dependencies are phase state, scratch state, zero-page ABI cells,
`c2_overlay_call` and `c2_overlay_call_range`; no new data owner is required.

The corrected far-service MAP tuple exposes only CPU block 3
(`$6000–$7fff`). The relocated body therefore remains able to see:

- E000 code in block 7, including both overlay-call helpers;
- C000 runtime/scratch state in block 6;
- I/O and the existing lower fixed facades.

It does not execute from a region that one of its own operations remaps. The
new facade entry has the already emitted nine-byte shape used by both existing
entries: enter the map, call one C body, then tail-transfer through the common
leave path. The established transitive ASM/C ABI and every-exit-unmap gates
remain mandatory; the study does not inherit their conclusions by prose.

## 4. The 54-byte floor

The floor is not a runtime tenant and does not protect a particular buffer.
It is an **owner-bound capacity reserve**. Its measurable effect is that no
successor card can silently spend the final 54 bytes of always-visible E000.
It was bound after append-final consolidation measured exactly 54 bytes
remaining, with an explicit rule that the next demand triggers scope triage
rather than a fourth erosion event.

Its numerical size is therefore historical policy, not a hardware constant.
Changing it would require all of the following, none of which this study asks
for or uses:

1. a new explicit owner decision;
2. a final-ELF born-derived inventory of every tenant and hole;
3. a named workload or failure model protected by the replacement floor;
4. full linked qualification and mutations that reject both hidden tenants and
   a floor different from the newly authorized value.

## 5. Ordered implementation plan

The relocation and the parked seam remain two different product experiments.

### Card R1 — relocate the abort driver

Move only the 134-byte body, consume nine explicit facade-padding bytes, and
make no other product change. Acceptance is all-or-nothing:

- final-ElfTruth proves one body, one owner and one new location;
- emitted facade relocations prove enter → body → leave, while the facade stays
  exactly 98 bytes;
- the decoded runtime tuple maps only block 3 and leaves blocks 6/7 visible;
- the transitive C/ASM ABI and all-exit unmap gates cover the new body;
- the far service is 1,382/1,499 or better and facade padding is at least 10;
- E000 free space is born-derived as at least 195, with floor still 54.

Any Red returns to the owner. It does not silently open the parked capture
seam.

### Card R2 — reopen the parked input-fidelity seam

Only after R1's final artifacts and gates are green, repeat the already priced
34+25 placement. Its three permanent placement/ordering/real-consumer gates
and the 94-event, forced-89-frame-collection loss model remain unchanged. The
final linked ELF must report at least 136 E000 bytes free after capture and at
least 82 above the unchanged floor.

This ordering makes the seam a card again: R1 proves the architectural room;
R2 spends exactly the previously authorized input-fidelity freight.

## 6. Negative gates

The study driver carries eleven mutations. They reject range-arithmetic free
space, a move below 52 bytes, either form of floor spending, service overflow,
facade growth, negative padding, a second abort caller, any map that hides
E000, using the floor as freight, and merging the two cards.
