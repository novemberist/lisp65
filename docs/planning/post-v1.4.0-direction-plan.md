# Post-v1.4.0 direction — prioritized work plan

Status: **owner-approved 2026-08-09.** Priority 1 (golden-layout
inversion) is finally parked after both the original card and the owner's
single exceptionless replacement stopped before WPLTO. Priority 2 is closed
at its honest desk boundary; Priority 3 is host/media-green at Link 93 and
awaits only its bundled hardware row. Design principle:
**maximum autonomous host runway, all
device needs converge on one bundled session at the end.**

## The strategic read

Three parked strands each wait on exactly one missing piece, and none
of those pieces needs hardware to build. Sequencing them host-first
means Codex works autonomously for the whole stretch, and the single
eventual device session harvests all three at once. The parity
library — the standing strategic promise — is the backbone: every
priority below either advances it or rides its infrastructure.

## Priority 1 — Golden-layout inversion (host-only)

The ownership programme's restart wisdom, now built: replace the
acceptance pipeline with **one self-contained comparison** of the
linked product card against a golden, SHA-bound layout artifact — no
checker vocabulary, no ordering, no meta-levels. The golden artifact
is produced once from the proven-fitting geometry (the 1.8/1.9 cards'
0-deficit layout) and reviewed once.

- Deliverable: the golden artifact + the single comparison gate +
  **one product card** under it.
- Green reopens, in order: 1.5 Halt 2 → parity pilot → **Link 91
  ready** (device row for the bundled session).
- Red parks the programme with the inversion also exhausted — a
  genuinely final answer either way.
- Why first: highest strategic value, purely autonomous, and its
  output defines the bundled session's main row.
