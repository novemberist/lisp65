## Ground rules

- Every permanent gate emits a positive execution witness
  (`executed_cases` plus a pinned `expected_cases`). Exit status zero without a
  nonzero, exact case count is not green; built-but-unrun variants fail the
  same canary.

Unchanged from the intervention regime: host-first, device demand collected
into **one** bundled session at the end, Class A + convicted-one-liner
delegation autonomous, first-red discipline, receipts, claim hygiene, and the
stuck-debugging rule (contradiction = reading task, not another capture).

Ordering rule: **measurement before mechanism, and freight that does not
depend on parked machinery outranks freight that does.**

## Phase I — Interrupt ownership (priority 2, bounded)

Status: **I1+I2 accepted as one unit; I3, the CRC relocation, WPLTO and
exactly one Link 76 are complete. Hardware effectiveness readback remains
bundled with the final Phase-V session.** Authority:
`config/c2-interrupt-ownership-policy.json`; review:
`c2.2-interrupt-ownership-halt2.md`.

I1. **Source inventory (paper).** Enumerate every interrupt source that can
    assert on this platform and classify each: masked by
    `c2_kernal_take_ownership`, structurally impossible, or **unhandled**.
    The completed exact-core inventory corrects the starting premise:
    F011/SD has no CPU-interrupt edge and Buffered-UART drives its nominal IRQ
    output inactive. The actual internal gaps are Ethernet, Auto-IEC and the
    CPU-internal Audio-DMA IRQ.
I2. **Policy contract.** For each unhandled source decide: mask at ownership
    time, acknowledge in the handler, or document as impossible with a
    citation. The source-less guard stays; what changes is that an unowned
    source can no longer *reach* it undiagnosed.
    Proposed policy: mask/ack Ethernet at `$D6E1`, Auto-IEC at `$D697` and
    Audio-DMA at `$D713` under `SEI`, prove all enable bits zero before
    `CLI`, retain raster as the sole owned IRQ, and exclude
    interrupt-generating cartridges from the supported C2-lite profile.
I3. **Product cut + WPLTO**, then one link. Expected cost: small, resident;
    the walls are known. The permanent source/ELF gate must reject omission,
    wrong-mask and wrong-register mutations for each source. The final
    bundled device session reads `$D6E1`, `$D697` and `$D713` after boot and
    proves the three enable fields masked.
    Source policy and the final 16/16 mutations are green. The first WPLTO
    stopped before its seed artifact: `c2_kernal_take_ownership` grew by
    exactly 42 bytes, making the exact-fit 289-byte handoff 331 bytes and
    overlap the pinned facade. The measured Class-C direction is to move the
    unchanged 108-byte, boot-only `c2k_crc16` body from the pinned handoff
    into ordinary resident text. The authorized fresh probe proved the real
    result: `c2k_crc16` is 108 bytes at `$A1DD`, has one whole-ELF caller,
    executes before ownership and is unreachable afterwards. The handoff is
    223/289 bytes. WPLTO retained 243 B text, the exact 54 B E000 floor,
    2 B fixed-block, 137 B BSS, 50 B island and 113 B session-family
    headroom. Exactly one product link followed; no retry or inherited green
    was claimed.
→ **Class-C halt #2:** contract review before the product cut.
   **Accepted 2026-07-28.** This remains robustness work on its own merits.
   If it also explains the parked red frame, that is a free by-product, not
   the justification.

## Device sessions

**One** bundled session, at the end, carrying: whatever I3 produces, the
V1/V2 smoke lines, and K2's hold as a trailing line. Instruments are
preloaded and blind polling is forbidden. First Red is dependency-scoped:
the failing row and every dependent row stop, while independent passive
observations and feature-disjoint rows continue in the same physical session
if the device remains live and defined and they cannot overwrite the failed
state. A red fail-closed frame, crash, corrupted state or undefined device
state remains terminal for the whole session.
