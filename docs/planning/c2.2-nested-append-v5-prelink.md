## C2J validation/reconstruction boundary

The authorized final C2J split is complete. `journal_validate` performs the
single 64-byte Bank-5 read, checks identity, generation, flags, bounds and
CRC, and leaves that exact snapshot in the already exclusive 304-byte phase
scratch. `journal_reconstruct` consumes only that snapshot; it cannot reread
Bank 5 and it rejects execution without the successful validation marker.

The snapshot is an alternate view of the existing `old_header[48]` plus the
first 16 bytes of `new_header`. It therefore adds no declared resident tuple,
no second storage truth and no check/use reread. Target-object sizes are 970 B
for validation and 1,176 B for reconstruction, respectively 822 B and 616 B
below the immutable 1,792-byte slice cap.

The target compiler emits a 13-byte `.noinit` static-stack diagnostic for the
relocatable reconstruction section. This is not a declared product BSS object:
llvm-mos assigns the static stack during whole-program LTO (Link 32's final
`.noinit` is zero bytes). Even the conservative relocatable number fits the
known 19-byte BSS remainder with 6 B. It is nevertheless recorded rather than
hidden; the successor product link must measure the actual BSS wall afresh.

## Serial driver and overlay closure

The resident driver is the only code allowed to load or call an append phase.
Its order is explicit:

- stage/publish: envelope through fronts, one reservation branch, journal
  write through entry publication, decode, header/publication, journal clear;
- normal transient return: fronts, rollback preparation, journal write,
  unpublish, finalize, journal clear;
- non-local abort: validate then reconstruct one active C2J, restore it, then
  remove every active transient high-tail record through the same serialized
  rollback phases.

The permanent closure gate inspects relocations for all 20 transported append
sections. A phase may call a helper in its own section, but a reference to a
different overlay section or to any overlay transport entry point is a hard
error. Four negative mutations prove both forbidden edge classes; resident
driver-to-overlay and same-section edges remain legal.

All 20 measured phases fit. The tightest are `publish_cells` at 1,730 B (62 B
headroom), `rollback_finalize` at 1,682 B (110 B) and
`reserve_transient` at 1,606 B (186 B).

## B2 — RUN/STOP uses the C2J landing

`lisp_abort_jump` is the sole non-local landing for ordinary errors and
RUN/STOP. Under C2D-v5 it closes an abandoned runtime-overlay transaction,
runs `c2_product_abort_cleanup`, and only then calls `longjmp`. There is no
RUN/STOP-specific rollback mechanism.

The permanent B2 fixture injects RUN/STOP at all 17 current mutation cutpoints
(persistent append, nested persistent append, transient publication and
transient removal) plus the quiescent-wrapper case. All 18 cases restore C2D,
Session Attic and exports byte-identically; the wrapper disappears completely,
while a previously committed persistent descendant remains present and
callable.
