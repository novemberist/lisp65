## Preferred next decision

Split the 1,862-byte middle phase once more at its natural boundary:

1. `journal_validate` reads the 64-byte C2J, proves magic/version,
   generation, flags, bounds and CRC, and retains that exact validated
   snapshot in the already exclusive 304-byte append scratch;
2. `journal_reconstruct` consumes only that snapshot to reconstruct the
   append state.

The existing `old_header[48]` plus 16 bytes of `new_header` can carry the
whole snapshot during this transition.  Reconstruction consumes it before
either header buffer is reused.  This adds **zero resident tuple bytes**,
performs no second Bank-5 read and therefore introduces no check/use gap
between validation and reconstruction.

The serial driver must enforce `validate → reconstruct`; reconstruction
without a successful validation marker is a format/state error.  No overlay
phase may call another overlay phase.  This follow-up is not authorized by
this receipt.

The Link-32 product remains the rollback line.  No C2J behavior, B2 fixture,
overlay-closure result, product identity, hardware behavior or latency is
