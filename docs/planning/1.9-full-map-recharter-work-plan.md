# 1.9 work plan — the full-map ownership recharter

Status: **commissioned 2026-08-06** (owner direction decision: maximum
autonomous host-side runway; this is the new owner-grounded charter
the 1.8 final-park edge requires). Reviewer charter; Codex phases it.

## The one sentence

Reopen the finally-parked full-map ownership programme under its own
edge condition — the complete verifier vocabulary inventoried and
gated **before** any card — and win the one product-shaped card whose
green reopens the parity chain (1.5 Halt 2 → parity pilot → Link 91
→ parity modules).

## Why this recommission is legitimate

The 1.8 edge demanded a new owner-grounded charter with the complete
closure vocabulary up front; the owner grounds it now by direction
choice. The case law is binding: the programme never failed on
capacity or placement (final card: 0 B deficit, 5 B margin) — it
failed twice on verifier vocabulary (seven sections; then ABI
policies plus a secondary driver-gate fault). This charter's first
page is therefore the vocabulary contract.

## The vocabulary contract (Phase A, the heart of the block)

One machine-readable closure of everything the acceptance path can
ever name: output sections (including relocation sections), ABI
policies for every assembler leaf (`c2_physical_read_converged` and
`vm_code_load_converged` are the convicted precedents), driver gates
(`fresh_replacement_gates` is the convicted precedent), owners, live
sets, and checker expectations. Completeness is proven by mutation:
for every vocabulary class, removing one member must turn the closure
red. No card runs while any acceptance checker can meet a token the
vocabulary does not know.

## Inherited substrate (all green, all binding)

The 1.5/1.7/1.8 contracts and live gates; the 874-byte equivalence-
gated assembly body; the ZP map; the ordinary chain `$B61D..$BFFB`
with its five-byte non-freight margin; the SHA-bound LTO replay
instrument (the free rehearsal); both red cards as failure
authorities.

## Bounds and autonomy profile

- Host and micro-ELF only; the one WPLTO card at the end; **no device
  contact anywhere in this block** (Link 91 belongs to the reopened
  pilot, a separate later decision).
- Codex runs all phases autonomously; reviewer acceptances are
  asynchronous and do not gate progress on the owner. **The single
  owner halt is the final card/reopening decision.**
- The 1.5/1.7 rejection rules verbatim; the one-card rule with the
  ownership-programme edge discipline: a red card of any kind parks
  the programme again, finally, with no third recommission implied.

## Phased execution

### Phase A — acceptance-vocabulary closure

Build the machine-readable vocabulary before any card directory exists.
The closure binds the exact terminal v1.8 ELF and both 190/190 inventory
receipts, derives the C-called assembler-leaf set from that ELF, and
enumerates every literal receipt token consumed by the old and new card
drivers.  Successful and failed internal receipts are a discriminated
union: a typed failure may never be consumed through
`fresh_replacement_gates`.

Exit: the permanent gate is in `check-source`; every vocabulary member
and every checker expectation is removed once and rejected.  No compile,
link, WPLTO or device contact occurs.

### Phase B — SHA-bound free rehearsal

Re-run the v1.8 Phase-C gate against the SHA-bound v1.7 LTO failure
object: two micro links and two product-object relinks, byteidentical
within each pair.  Apply the completed 190-member section inventory and
the exact assembler ABI audit to both terminal v1.8 ELFs.  Rehearse the
failure/success receipt union without consuming a success-only field.

Exit: a permanent replay receipt authorizes exactly one card, and records
zero product compiles, zero fresh WPLTO and zero device contacts.

### Phase C — one-shot preflight

Require green Phase A and B receipts, absence of every card/First-Red
output, the SHA-bound historical First Red, and a fresh Ship witness.
The preflight may build host-side witness material, but it must record
zero WPLTO and must not create the card build directory.  Its receipt
binds the plan and the sole-card driver.

### Phase D — the sole product-shaped card

Run exactly one fresh WPLTO in the fully owned geometry.  On green, bind
the exact ordinary chain, five-byte non-freight margin, 190-section
inventory, 98-byte facade, 874-byte far body, 6/12-byte stack arena and
the completed ABI/driver closure.  On any red, write only the typed
primary diagnostic and available artifacts; never touch success-only
receipt fields.  A red parks finally and authorizes no retry.

### Owner halt — reopening decision

No Link 91 or device work occurs in this charter.  A green card reaches
the sole owner halt and asks whether to reopen 1.5 Halt 2, the preserved
parity pilot and Link 91.  A red reaches the same halt only as the final
park record.
