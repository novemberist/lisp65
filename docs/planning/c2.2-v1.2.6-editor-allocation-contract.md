# v1.2.6 editor allocation contract

Status: **binding before implementation, 2026-07-31**.

## Boundary

The editor may allocate while editing and painting, but no physical key class
may consume a nursery by itself on average.  The permanent lane measures the
generated product IDE composition and distinguishes:

- an ordinary printable key;
- the key that crosses the 79-column fill boundary;
- a cursor key that changes the visible scroll window.

For every route and class, mean allocations are at most 191 cells.  No one key
allocates more than 193 cells, which proves at most one collection for every
one of the 192 possible incoming nursery phases.  A result below the mean but
with a multi-collection spike is therefore still red.

The limits are allocation limits, not timing claims.  The separately measured
89-frame collection envelope explains why they matter on the target.

## Coalescing contract

The product may drain at most the ten characters that fit in the KERNAL typed
queue before repainting.  It consumes those characters in observation order,
does not make their edits visible out of order, and repaints only after the
observed queue is empty.  The editor-exit marker stops draining before another
key is consumed.

Physical RUN/STOP is not a typed editor event.  It remains the out-of-band
evaluation abort owned by `lisp_poll`; it is never queued, delayed or
coalesced by the editor.

## Product shape and stop rule

The repair belongs to the Bank-2 IDE composition.  It may not add a resident
byte or fuse a Session correctness phase.  The old three-service G1 carrier is
not part of this work.  If the allocation ceiling cannot be met under those
conditions, the result returns as a priced owner decision rather than moving
a wall.

The machine contract is
`config/c2-v126-editor-allocation-contract.json`.  The live gate runs the
80-key serial and ten-key-coalesced lanes, a five-key scroll lane, every
incoming nursery phase, the source-level coalescing closure, and at least six
negative mutations.  A positive execution witness is mandatory.
