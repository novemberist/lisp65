# v1.5.0 release work plan

Status: **Halt #1 owner-accepted 2026-08-18; Phase E open.** The final
Link-116 product/name-freight pair passed the complete D1–D5 hardware session,
the user-headroom contract and the same-device boot comparison. The owner chose
to ship the measured 36-second boot. Product bytes are frozen; documentation,
assets and double readback now lead to Halt #2.

## The one sentence

Deliver the era's harvest as v1.5.0 — `trace`/`untrace` on the
function-cell ABI, `defstruct` under the shadow guard, the REPL
direct path, boot liveness and require echo — with the new
performance-smoke duty making sure nothing seconds-scale ever ships
unmeasured again.

## Freight (scope-frozen at commissioning)

1. **`trace`/`untrace`** into `inspect` (hardware-proven exact BCODE
   restoration; Link-95/96 lineage).
2. **`defstruct`** under the terminal-ingress shadow guard —
   delivered with honest notes: sequence timing (priced), the
   guard's existence and its log semantics.
3. **REPL direct path** — the interactive vocabulary at direct cost;
   the ceremony's 60/62-frame price documented as the architecture
   statement (durability does real work).
4. **Experience pillars**: boot liveness lines, require echo.
5. **Editor known-issues softening** (carried from the 1.12 booking).

## Build rules

- Candidate build activates the v1.5 opt-in seams explicitly;
  historical worlds stay byteidentical.
- Producer discipline throughout: SHA-bound input closures, published
  callees, media-pair identity (same world, not same bytes),
  contract members regenerate with any payload change.
- One product card under the standing discipline; a red returns as an
  owner disposition.

## Phases

- **A — frozen freight and producer closure:** materialize the successor
  stdlib from the accepted direct-expression and require-intent sources,
  bind the regular `WORKBENCH 1.5.0` banner, enumerate every product/link/
  media input by SHA-256, and prove that historical Link-95/96 worlds remain
  byteidentical.  No product link runs in A.
- **B — linker-free release preflight:** close published callees, exact
  surface membership, terminal-return-guard coverage, Experience opt-ins,
  performance bounds and same-world library inputs over the fresh static
  plane.  Bind the resulting Bank-2/profile geometry before the card.
- **C — the one product card and media closure:** run exactly one WPLTO,
  complete its artifacts without relinking, regenerate every medium contract
  member from the resulting product world, and read every role back.  Any red
  stops at the owner disposition; no retry is implicit.
- **D — one bundled device session:** execute D1 through D5 below in order.
  D5 is release-terminal on any bound performance-wall violation.  Stop at
  Halt #1 with the complete stopped-state/session receipt.
- **E — release closure:** only after Halt #1, promote documentation and
  notes, build and twice read back the four release assets, verify the regular
  banner identity, then stop at Halt #2 for the publish word.
