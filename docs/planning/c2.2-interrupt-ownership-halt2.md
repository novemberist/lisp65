# Phase I Halt #2 — complete interrupt ownership

Status: **owner accepted as one unit; product cut authorized**

Authority: `config/c2-interrupt-ownership-policy.json`.

## Result first

The exact tested-core graph corrects the old R-1 premise. F011/SD is not an
IRQ source on this core, and neither is the Buffered-UART despite its
interrupt-looking registers. The complete internal ownership gap is three
families:

| family | events | current state | proposed ownership action |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Ethernet `$D6E1` | RX queue, TX complete | enabled state inherited from firmware; not touched | write `$00`: disable both masks; status may remain pending but cannot assert |
| Auto-IEC `$D697` | RX, ready, timeout | enabled state inherited from firmware; not touched | write `$F0`: clear four status bits, disable master and all three events |
| Audio-DMA `$D713` | channels 0–3 | enabled state inherited from firmware; not touched | write `$00`: clear four flags and four enables |

All three writes occur under `SEI`, after the MEGA65 I/O personality is
selected and before the owned raster IRQ is enabled. Readback must prove the
enable fields are zero before `CLI`. No new device handler is proposed:
VIC-raster remains the sole owned IRQ, and the source-less Freezer episode
guard remains unchanged.

## Why this inventory is complete

The tested R6 core is `03b24c6b9d0e456f762fdca0d2dd66ec3c3e1fc6`,
the core identity used by the accepted hardware receipts.

The top-level graph is closed:

```text
IRQ = R6 external line
    & cartridge /IRQ
    & VIC IRQ
    & (CIA1 & Ethernet & Buffered-UART & Auto-IEC)
    + CPU-internal Audio-DMA irq_pending

NMI = R6 external line
    & cartridge /NMI
    & CIA2 NMI
    & RESTORE NMI
```

The generic R6 external IRQ/NMI signals are tied inactive. Cartridge pins are
forwarded separately and have no device-independent acknowledgement. The
proposed supported profile therefore excludes interrupt-generating
cartridges; passive cartridges are not claimed or modified. This exclusion is
user-visible in `docs/known-issues.md`: one foreign/source-less IRQ per raster
episode remains tolerated for a legitimate Freezer return, while a held or
repeating cartridge interrupt storm is unsupported and deliberately
fail-closed.

The closure comes directly from the official sources:

- [`machine.vhdl` combines IRQ/NMI](https://github.com/MEGA65/mega65-core/blob/03b24c6b9d0e456f762fdca0d2dd66ec3c3e1fc6/src/vhdl/machine.vhdl#L858-L867);
- [`iomapper.vhdl` names the four I/O IRQ contributors](https://github.com/MEGA65/mega65-core/blob/03b24c6b9d0e456f762fdca0d2dd66ec3c3e1fc6/src/vhdl/iomapper.vhdl#L703-L706);
- [`mega65r6.vhdl` adds the cartridge pins](https://github.com/MEGA65/mega65-core/blob/03b24c6b9d0e456f762fdca0d2dd66ec3c3e1fc6/src/vhdl/mega65r6.vhdl#L1345-L1347);
- [`gs4510.vhdl` implements the CPU-internal Audio-DMA IRQ](https://github.com/MEGA65/mega65-core/blob/03b24c6b9d0e456f762fdca0d2dd66ec3c3e1fc6/src/vhdl/gs4510.vhdl#L4528-L4554).

Every leaf of those cones is classified in the JSON authority. Blocks outside
the cones cannot independently reach the vectors.

## Corrections produced by the inventory

### F011/SD is not an IRQ source

The chipset reference's physical PDF page 143 says that the F011 IRQ status
exists but interrupts are not implemented on the 45GS27. The exact core
agrees: F011/SD has no input in the `io_irq` expression. A mounted D81 can
affect workload and media state, but cannot directly produce the source-less
CPU IRQ assumed by the parked R-1 note.

### Buffered-UART is not an IRQ source on this core

`$D0E0/$D0E1` expose enable and status-looking fields, but the bound RTL drives
the module IRQ output high unconditionally and never asserts it. This is a
structural exclusion, not a reason to spend product bytes on a mask.

### Audio-DMA is an IRQ source

The chipset PDF says the opposite on physical page 99. The exact tested-core
RTL is authoritative for this product: `$D713` contains four enables and four
flags, and an enabled channel can set CPU `irq_pending`. This discrepancy is
recorded rather than normalized away. Product use of Audio-DMA is not required
for the gap to matter: firmware or the hypervisor may leave an enable armed
before ownership. That is the first named mechanism capable of explaining the
parked `defstruct` red frame without product participation, but it is not a
claim that the mechanism caused that observation.

## Existing sources already closed

- VIC raster is owned and acknowledged. Sprite collision sources are masked;
  lightpen and raster-X masks have no write path and remain zero in the bound
  core.
- CIA1's timer A, timer B, TOD alarm, serial and FLAG sources are masked and
  acknowledged at `$DC0D`.
- CIA2's corresponding NMI sources are masked and acknowledged at `$DD0D`;
  timer A is additionally stopped.
- RESTORE/Freezer and its possible deferred return IRQ remain under the proven
  raster-delimited episode contract.
- Normal DMAgic completion, classic C65 UART, keyboard queue, SID/FM/audio
  mixer, RTC and the 1541 core have no independent edge into the exact graph.
- `BRK` is vector ingress rather than a device source. The successor gate must
  reject any decoded reachable `BRK`; scanning raw zero bytes is not proof.

## Proposed contract

In `c2_kernal_take_ownership`, under the already present `SEI`:

1. normalize the MEGA65 I/O personality;
2. perform the existing CIA/VIC mask and acknowledgements;
3. write `$00` to `$D6E1`, `$F0` to `$D697`, and `$00` to `$D713`;
4. prove `$D6E1 & $C0 == 0`, `$D697 & $0F == 0`, and
   `$D713 & $0F == 0`;
5. publish/map/verify the owned window as today;
6. acknowledge VIC, enable raster only, then `CLI`.

Status bits are allowed to change after the writes. The proof concerns enable
bits: with every enable zero, those devices cannot pull the IRQ line low.

The product does not acknowledge a foreign device after vector ownership.
It prevents all software-maskable internal foreign sources from asserting in
the first place. Under the supported no-interrupting-cartridge profile and
the reachable-`BRK` gate, a source-less IRQ has only the already-contracted
Freezer/hypervisor return meaning; the consecutive-entry storm guard remains
fail-closed.

## Gates required before any link

- exact source-cone equality: every core IRQ/NMI leaf occurs once in the
  inventory;
- the three writes are after I/O personality selection, under `SEI`, before
  raster enable and `CLI`;
- enable-field readback precedes raster enable;
- omission, wrong mask and wrong-register mutations for every new family;
- mutations that classify Audio-DMA as impossible or F011 as live must fail;
- a decoded reachable `BRK` mutation must fail;
- all current raster, Freezer-episode, Z-boundary, KERNAL-freedom and window
  gates replay unchanged.

Capacity is deliberately not estimated here. If the policy is accepted, one
fresh WPLTO measures the resident cost against the sealed geometry.

## Owner decision — accepted 2026-07-28

The owner accepted the strict policy as a unit: mask Ethernet, Auto-IEC and
Audio-DMA at ownership; keep VIC-raster as the sole owned IRQ and keep the
Freezer episode guard; exclude interrupt-generating cartridges; bind F011/SD
and Buffered-UART as structural non-sources on the tested core.

Implementation conditions are binding:

1. publish the cartridge-storm exclusion in the Known-Issues register;
2. reject one-source-omitted mutations and carry a bundled hardware line that
   reads `$D6E1`, `$D697` and `$D713` after boot;
3. add the Audio-DMA PDF/RTL contradiction to the upstream owner bundle with
   the exact RTL lines; and
4. correct parked R-1: F011 is excluded, and firmware/hypervisor-inherited
   Audio-DMA enable state is a plausible but unproven red-frame mechanism.

No claim is made that this closes the parked `defstruct` red frame. It is
independently justified ownership hardening.

## WPLTO First Red — fixed handoff

The accepted source cut and its 14/14 source/policy mutations are green, but
the single WPLTO attempt stopped at the resident-island seed link before a
product-shaped artifact existed. The fixed handoff is exactly 289 bytes in
Link 75. The three masks plus effectiveness readback grew
`c2_kernal_take_ownership` from 84 to 126 bytes, making the section 331 bytes
and overlapping the pinned facade by 42 bytes.

The measured Class-C location candidate is the unchanged 108-byte
`c2k_crc16`: it is boot-only, has one direct caller, and has no E000 or fixed
domain operand. Moving that body to ordinary resident text projects a
223-byte handoff with 66 bytes headroom, while consuming 108 of the
predecessor's measured 351 ordinary-text bytes. This is a projection, not a
green map; it requires a separately authorized fresh WPLTO and every
pre-ownership, handoff, profile-data, KERNAL-freedom and ownership-dataflow
gate. No facade move, reanchor, product link or second WPLTO is implied.

## Authorized relocation, green WPLTO and Link 76

The owner authorized the boot-only relocation. `c2k_crc16` now enters the
ordinary `.text` output from `.text.c2_kernal_boot_only`; its linked body is
108 bytes at `$A1DD`. The final ELF proves one direct edge from
`c2_kernal_take_ownership`, address order before the handoff, no
post-ownership reachability and no call from any other owner.

The fresh WPLTO therefore closes the former First Red rather than projecting
past it:

- fixed handoff: 223/289 bytes, 66 bytes free;
- bank-0 text headroom: 243 bytes;
- `$E000` headroom: 54 bytes, exactly the authorized floor;
- fixed hot block / ordinary BSS / island: 2 / 137 / 50 bytes;
- session family: 65,423 bytes, 113 bytes free;
- interrupt source/placement/policy mutations: 16/16 rejected.

Exactly one successor link was performed. Link 76 is
`569cb2496aa0d251e989a31edf2015880599029a7b6ccb69c8a9c6d3b5373343`;
artifact completion used zero compiler and zero linker runs. The three
post-boot register readbacks remain unclaimed and travel in the final bundled
Phase-I/Phase-V/K2 hardware session.
