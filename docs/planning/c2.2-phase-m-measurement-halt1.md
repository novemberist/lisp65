## Result in one paragraph

The current valid `require 'defstruct` hardware observations are **12 seconds
first and 9 seconds on the idempotent repeat**, not 30 seconds. The repeat
still executes 98.55% of the first run's VM instructions and 96.24% of its
Prim-67 reads, so the measured duplicated work is parse/validation/resolution,
not library publication. The final accepted GC observation is **89 frames for
one collection and 96 contract root-block reads**; the earlier 82-frame row was
one workload containing two collections and never supported an isolated
per-collection claim. Static/captured attribution proves at least 576 DMA
jobs in the measured collector shape, but existing receipts contain no phase
timestamps, so assigning the 89 frames to roots, symbols, graph trace, arena,
or sweep would be fabrication.

## M2 — GC envelope

Corrected hardware ledger:

| Identity | Workload frames | Collections | Contract block reads | Claim |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Link 57 | 82 | 2 | 192 | no isolated collection time |
| Link 66 | 88 | 1 | 96 | informative, no limit |
| final G5 | 89 | 1 | 96 | informative, no limit |

The final-G5 captures bind 480 symbols. The measured high-edge workload binds
1,536 C2D roots, read in 96 blocks of 32 bytes. Current source therefore
forces this lower bound before external graph traversal, arena movement, or
sweep:

- 96 C2D root reads, 32 bytes each;
- 480 `symval` reads, 2 bytes each;
- 0–480 additional `symfn` reads, depending on the uncaptured pointer bitmap;
- an uncaptured number of external-cell reads/writes and arena copies.

Thus **96 is not the total DMA-job count**. The proven lower bound is 576
jobs and 4,032 payload bytes. Other exact geometry is 47 usable hot cells,
1,024 external cells, a 134-byte mark bitmap, and a 128-entry shadow-root
capacity.

The collector already exposes phase boundaries `LA(17)`, `LA(18)`, `LA(19)`
and `LA(20)`. They prove order, but no accepted run timestamped them.
Consequently the following target times remain unknown: shadow/C2D roots,
symbol roots, fixed-point trace, arena compaction, and sweep.

## Proposed single owner disposition

**Commission the measured `require` idempotence cut; do not commission a GC
mechanism yet.**

The proposed `require` cut has this budget and exit condition:

- Bank-2-only; **0 resident bytes**, no wall or Session-store negotiation;
- at most **512 Bank-2 bytes**;
- first-load validation, dependency order, resolution lock, generation binding,
  and rollback unchanged;
- already-loaded lane must reduce both VM instructions and Prim-67 reads by at
  least **90%** against 134,788/384;
- hardware wall measurement rides only in the already planned final bundled
  session.

For GC, commission only measurement instrumentation at the existing phase
seam, carried in that same final bundled session. It must bind:

- frame deltas 17→18, 18→19, 19→20;
- pointer-valued `symfn` read count;
- external mark interval and fixed-point pass count;
- arena bytes copied;
- dead external cells written during sweep.

Only those numbers may select a GC mechanism or byte budget. This keeps the
ordering rule literal: the measured duplicated resolver work may be cut now;
the collector, whose internal time distribution is still unknown, may not.
