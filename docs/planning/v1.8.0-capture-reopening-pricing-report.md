# v1.8 Block 2 — Capture/Hybrid reopening price

Recorded: 2026-08-27

Scope: host-only inventory and pricing; no product link, medium or device

## Result

The sealed native Capture/Hybrid substrate fits the published v1.7 product
world.  There is no symbol, E000 or composed-Bank-2 capacity blocker.  This is
not yet a product or losslessness claim: the two product features remain
inactive in the published profile, and the only atomic activation owner is
still the deferred Comfort library.

The mechanically isolated candidate is deliberately smaller than Comfort:

- `LISP65_V160_INPUT_CAPTURE`, replacing the base IRQ source with
  `src/optional/c2_kernal_input_capture.s`;
- `LISP65_V160_INPUT_HYBRID`, adding
  `src/optional/c2_kernal_input_consumer.s`;
- three E000 sections and their three relocation sections;
- the existing 112-byte `repl.buf` lifetime alias, split into 108 ring index
  values and four monotone counters;
- the already-shipped evaluator queue-owner guard.

Balanced multiline input, history, prompt, framebuffer ownership, the
Block-3 editor and the whole `repl-comfort` library are not members of this
price.

The sealed final ELF still executes as follows:

- 94 events captured and drained in order, zero drops, event six present;
- 107 usable ring events, leaving 13 above the 94-event wall;
- raw PETSCII normalization 512/512 across private modes 2 and 3, including

The Capture source, scalar consumer, `lisp_poll` guard and atomic
arm/disarm owner are byte-identical to the accepted Phase-1b seal.  The live
source-level counter and single-owner gates pass today.

## Known blockers and world split

1. **No final product exists.** This block has no WPLTO/link authority.  The
   current product-profile projector can materialize both features and both
   source owners at the real `single_link` seam, but only a future authorized
   card can prove their final consumption.
2. **Activation belongs to Comfort.** The sole bound origin closes the tail,
   zeros head and all four counters, then arms by writing tail zero.  It lives
   in `repl-comfort`, which is excluded here.  The product substrate is inert
   until a later accepted client owns this lifecycle.
3. **Public `key-event` while armed remains the registered v1.7 question.**
   Modes 0/1 legitimately read the hardware queue.  The intended lifecycle
   avoids the collision by proving Capture closed before user evaluation;
   that proof is mandatory before a user-visible losslessness claim.  The
   product substrate price does not pretend the public path was redirected.
4. **The live editor is a different world.** Block 3 added `%rl-poll` and
   related helpers after the accepted Comfort seal.  Running the historical
   full-Hybrid gate against that live source correctly fails with
   `function not in directory: %rl-poll`.  Block 2 must not repair this by
   silently compiling Comfort or by substituting the live editor for sealed
   evidence.

## Recommended next card

One product-substrate reopening card can enable exactly the two native
features, with no Comfort or diagnostic freight.  It needs explicit authority
for one WPLTO and one product link.  Before qualification it must prove real
compiler-process consumption of both features/sources, the three derived E000
placements, the composed Bank-2/MAP map, final-ELF 94/94 and 512/512 walls,
queue single ownership, and Capture closure before public evaluation.

The output of that card would be a host-qualified substrate, not hardware
acceptance and not permission to reopen Comfort.  Comfort remains gated behind
both v1.8 opening blocks.

The derivation is executable with
`tools/host-lisp/c2_v18_capture_reopening_pricing.py check`; its mutation set
runs with `selftest`.  No receipt for a new product world is sealed on
purpose: this block produces a reopening price, not a candidate pair.
